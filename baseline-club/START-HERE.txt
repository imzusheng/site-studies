Baseline Club — Web Tennis / 2.0.0-rc2

Open index.html in a modern WebGL2 browser. The delivered HTML is self-contained and does not require Internet access.

PLAY
- Choose one of three opponents from the home screen, or enter ball-machine training.
- WASD / arrow keys: move. Hold Space: charge. Release: swing.
- P: pause. R: restart. J: replay the previous shot. Escape: leave replay/dialogs.
- On touch devices use the joystick and HIT button.

SETTINGS
- Physics-derived landing is the default. Manual target mode remains optional.
- Difficulty changes pace, width, depth, spin, opponent response and timing tolerance without changing gravity.
- Predicted bounce, recommended position, trajectory, timing cue, auto movement and timing assist are independent switches.
