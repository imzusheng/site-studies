The official character pages state CC-BY and require the credits above.
Their CC-BY license link is https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .
The license, rather than this description, governs reuse. No endorsement by
Blender Studio or Blender Foundation is implied.

Adaptations made for this game: export to glTF/GLB; consolidation of the
production control rig into an 18-joint runtime deformation rig; selected
subdivision and topology; diffuse-texture atlas conversion; material and
outfit colors; a baked hand-pose adaptation, racket socket, limited retarget
and grip corrections. Full production facial controls, corrective shapes
and Blender scripts do not execute in the browser. This is a real-time
adaptation, not a claim of feature parity with the original production rig.

The artist source receipt and SHA-256 values are retained in assets-source/.
The local GLBs are derived from those cached sources. No paid model pack,
user tracking or remote character download is needed at runtime.

Six full source recordings are cached locally under assets-source/full:
- lvargas_Derecha_4seg.bvh
- adorozco_Reves_8seg.bvh
- lvargas_VDerecha.bvh
- lvargas_VReves.bvh
- lvargas_Servicio_21seg.bvh
- lvargas_Remate.bvh

The game uses a bounded excerpt from each recording, baked at 50 Hz plus
exact start/contact marks. It does not play the whole multi-stroke file as
one clip. All source files are retained; hashes, declared/read row counts,
and excerpt lengths are recorded in assets-source/motion-receipts.json.

Changes: selected single-stroke intervals, normalized initial heading,
scaled positions, continuous quaternion signs, adaptation to Rain/Snow,
time scaling, grounding and limited non-dominant-hand IK. Reach and slice
are programmatic derivatives of the base strokes, not two new independent
recordings. Locomotion is an authored/procedural movement layer.
