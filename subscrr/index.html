FAQ

Good questions.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No — and it never will. You add subscriptions yourself in about a minute, so nothing about your finances ever leaves your hands.

Where is my data stored?

In your own private iCloud, tied to your Apple ID and synced across your devices. Our servers never receive the list, so we couldn't read it even if we tried.

Do you track me?

Not your money. Anonymous counters record things like "opened the app" or "added a subscription" so we know which screen to improve. No names, no amounts, no subscription list. Details live in the privacy policy.

What do I get for free?

Six subscription slots with full breakdowns, reminders, custom icons and iCloud sync. When six stops being enough, Premium lifts the limit and unlocks the receipt scanner.

How does AI Spend handle my receipts?

The AI reads the numbers and forgets the picture: values are extracted, the photo is discarded, and only hashes stay behind — never the images.

Can I cancel Premium anytime?

Yes, two taps — it's a standard store subscription. Your free plan keeps working afterwards. No hard feelings.
